Drop the photography here, then point `lib/photos.ts` at the files.

Suggested shapes:
- Arch slots (origins, boutique, maison, dfou3, coffrets) are cropped to 3:4
  or 4:5 portrait and masked into the maison's arch. Keep the subject centred
  and leave headroom — the top of the frame curves away.
- Export at ~1600px on the long edge, JPEG or WebP.
